ABOUT ME

I'm Eva Daniel, a technology professional at JPMorgan and a master's student in computer science at Georgia Tech, where I'm focusing on human-computer interaction.

My path into tech started with a wide curiosity about how things work. I grew up wanting to be a paleontologist, then an inventor, and eventually found my way into computer science, cybersecurity, and user-centered digital work.

At the University of Georgia, I studied computer science and earned a certificate in New Media. Along the way, I worked in IT, contributed to UGA CyberArch, helped found Women in Cybersecurity at UGA, and served as a business operations leader for the UGA Society for Cybersecurity.

After internships with Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan, I joined JPMorgan full time in 2025. Today, I'm building on that foundation through graduate study at Georgia Tech, where I'm focused on the intersection of software, systems, usability, and thoughtful digital experiences.
