Simplifying genograms, amplifying insights.

THE PROJECT

Genograms map family relationships and behavioral patterns across generations. GKnow reimagined a process often completed with pen and paper as a focused digital workspace for clinical use.

The product was designed to combine genogram creation with patient information management, giving therapists one place to build diagrams, record context, and return to their work over time.

MY ROLE

I served as a co-developer on a five-person interdisciplinary team alongside project management, creative direction, UX/UI, and development roles.

Our work moved from exploratory and user research through interface design, application development, testing, documentation, and final client handoff.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

The team researched professional, patient, and hobbyist audiences to understand how genograms were being created and where a digital tool could be most useful.

That research pointed to the strongest initial opportunity in clinical settings, shaping the product around therapists, patient workflows, and clearer communication between appointments.

CORE EXPERIENCE

  • Drag-and-drop tools for building detailed family genograms
  • Note-taking and patient information in a unified workspace
  • Security decisions shaped around HIPAA requirements
  • An expanded set of family roles, relationships, and connections

HANDOFF

The final delivery included the application source, a visual design guide, a UX map, and client documentation for future maintenance and development.

The roadmap identified opportunities such as Apple Pencil support, Face ID, client sharing, and more flexible control over layered family connections.
