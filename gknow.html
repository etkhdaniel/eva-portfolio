GKNOW / 2024

Simplifying genograms, amplifying insights.

GKnow is an iOS workspace for therapists to securely create, store, and edit detailed patient genograms.

Our team translated a complex, often hand-drawn clinical process into a focused digital experience built around relationships, context, and continuity of care.

TIME
2024 CAPSTONE

MY ROLE

THE PROBLEM

Complex family systems are difficult to capture with tools that are fragmented, inflexible, or still rooted in pen and paper.

Genograms help clinical professionals visualize relationships and patterns across generations. Our research found that many professionals still create them by hand, making it harder to revise a diagram, connect it to patient notes, and return to that information over time.

92.3%

of surveyed professionals in the team's research reported using genograms in their work.

THE SOLUTION

One secure workspace where therapists can build a genogram, record context, and manage patient work together.

GKnow centers the diagram without separating it from the information that gives it meaning. The iPad experience was designed to make complex relationships easier to create, understand, update, and discuss.

KEY FEATURES

A focused toolkit for clinical genograms.

  1. 01

    DRAG-AND-DROP BUILDER

    Create family structures with a visual toolset of people, relationships, and connection types.

  2. 02

    NOTES WITH CONTEXT

    Keep relevant observations and patient details connected to the people represented in a genogram.

  3. 03

    SECURE CLINICAL WORKFLOW

    Shape storage and access decisions around the privacy expectations of healthcare work.

  4. 04

    UNIFIED PATIENT MANAGEMENT

    Organize multiple patients and their genograms in a single, consistent workspace.

DISCOVERY RESEARCH

Finding the audience with the clearest need.

We explored three possible audiences: clinical professionals, patients, and genealogy hobbyists. The research pointed us toward therapist-led use, where genograms were already familiar and a digital workflow could have an immediate impact.

  1. 01

    ESTABLISHED BEHAVIOR

    Professionals already understood genograms and regularly used them as part of clinical work.

  2. 02

    AN ANALOG GAP

    Hand-drawn workflows revealed an opportunity for faster editing, cleaner organization, and easier revisiting.

  3. 03

    SHARED UNDERSTANDING

    A clearer visual tool could support conversations between therapists and patients during care.

TARGET USER

Clinical and family therapists who need to document changing relationships without losing the larger story.

MY ROLE

Co-developing an interdisciplinary capstone.

I worked as one of two developers on a five-person team alongside creative direction, project management, and UX/UI design. I helped turn the team's research and interface decisions into the working iOS experience.

The project moved through research, product definition, interface design, implementation, testing, documentation, and client handoff. That process taught me how development decisions shape the usability and clarity of an experience, especially when the subject matter is complex.

FINAL PRODUCT

A working foundation for a more intuitive genogram workflow.

The final delivery included the application source, visual design guide, UX map, and supporting documentation for future maintenance and development.
